                                          Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation




                    Press note on release of Expert Group Report on
                            Comprehensive Updation of CPI

                                              Posted On: 29 JAN 2026 4:00PM by PIB Delhi

        The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) is releasing,
        through this press note, the Report of the Expert Group on Comprehensive Updatio
        n of the Consumer Price Index (CPI). Expert Group, constituted for base revision of
        CPI, has experts from eminent academic institutions, seniors officers from Reserve
        Bank of India and Government of India. To accurately capture the changing
        consumption behaviour of the households and to incorporate latest global best
        practices including methodological developments, the Expert Group extensively
        deliberated on all aspects of the CPI series in its meetings. The report outlines the
        processes adopted for the base revision exercise, details the enhancements and
        modifications introduced in the new CPI series i.e. in CPI 2024, and provides detailed
        recommendations of the Expert Group.
        The major recommendations of the Expert Group include adoption of latest
        Classification of Individual Consumption According to Purpose (COICOP) 2018
        framework up to the subclass level for classification of items, updation of CPI item
        basket and their corresponding weights using Household Consumption Expenditure
        Survey(HCES) 2023-24, use of latest technology for price data collection and
        compilation, inclusion of administrative and online/e-commerce data, methodological
        improvements in index compilation including house rent index and more granular
        data dissemination.
        In CPI 2024 series, the item basket will comprise 358 weighted items, mapped to 12
        Divisions, 43 Groups, 62 Classes and 192 Sub-classes in accordance with the COICOP
        2018 classification. The structure of CPI 2024 series and weights at different levels of
        classification are provided in the report.
        The release of this report aims to facilitate the users of CPI with better
        understanding and assimilation of CPI 2024 series before its release on 12th February
        2026. The first release will provide indices from January 2025 onwards and inflation
        data for January 2026. The All-India level back series for the Rural, Urban, and
        Combined sectors, from January 2013 onwards, will also be released on 12th February,
        2026.
