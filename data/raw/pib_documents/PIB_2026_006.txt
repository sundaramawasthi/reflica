                                          Ministry of Statistics & Programme Implementation




          Pre-Release Consultative Workshop on Base Revision of
             GDP, IIP and CPI scheduled to be held at Chennai
                                 tomorrow

                                              Posted On: 29 JAN 2026 1:18PM by PIB Delhi


         MoSPI is poised to convene a Pre-Release Consultative Workshop on the Base
         Revision of GDP, IIP and CPI on 30 January, 2026 at Chennai, bringing together
         key stakeholders for informed deliberations
        The Ministry of Statistics and Programme Implementation (MoSPI) is convening the
        third Pre-Release Consultative Workshop on the base revision of the Gross Domestic
        Product (GDP), Index of Industrial Production (IIP) and Consumer Price Index (CPI) on
        30 January 2026 at Chennai. This workshop follows the successful conduct of the first
        and second consultative workshops held earlier in Mumbai in November, 2025 and
        New Delhi in December 2025, respectively. The objective of the series of workshops
        is to present the proposed methodological refinements and structural changes
        envisaged in the ongoing base revision of GDP, IIP and CPI, and to solicit feedback
        and suggestions from stakeholders.
        The deliberations will also focus on the introduction of new data sources and the
        adoption of advanced technological tools proposed to be used in the compilation of
        these key macroeconomic indicators. The workshop will bring together a wide and
        diverse group of participants, including representatives from the World Bank and
        other international organizations, the Reserve Bank of India, eminent economists,
        experts from financial institutions and the banking sector, subject-matter specialists,
        key users of official statistics, and senior officials from the Central and State
        Governments. Participation from such a broad spectrum of stakeholders is expected
        to enrich the discussions and enhance users’ understanding of the proposed
        changes in the revised series.
        The programme will commence with an inaugural session featuring addresses by Dr.
        C. Rangarajan, Chairman, Madras School of Economics, former Chairman, Economic
        Advisory Council to the Prime Minister and Ex-Governor, Reserve Bank of India, Prof.
        Rajeeva Laxman Karandikar, Chairman, National Statistical Commission, MoSPI, Dr.
        Saurabh Garg, Secretary, MoSPI, Dr. Shamika Ravi, member of the Economic
        Advisory Council to the Prime Minister and Shri N. K. Santoshi, Director General
        (Central Statistics). This will be followed by technical sessions on GDP, IIP and CPI,
