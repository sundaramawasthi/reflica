circular_dependency | conflicting_rules | non_numeric_value | unit_mismatch |
contradicts_current_state | invalid_target | unclear_which_entity | no_unique_best_choice
Use "needs_human_review" instead of "cannot_answer" when a provisional answer exists but a person should confirm it.
