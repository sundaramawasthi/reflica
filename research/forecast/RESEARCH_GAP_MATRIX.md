## 2. Closest prior work

- **Part A:** ForecastBench (#1), Forecast-Dojo (#3) and LiveMacroEval (#2). Live or replayed LLM forecasting with Brier scoring already exists, so an India focus is at most a **coverage and evaluation** contribution [U].
- **Part B:** LiveMacroEval (#2; US CPI, LLM agents revising live), Forecast-Dojo (#3; claim ledger), Mohan et al. (#7; strong India numeric baseline), Kalamara et al. (#12; text features). Gap v6 is provisional (`../../docs/research_archive/research_gaps/CLOSEST_METHODS.md`).

## 3. Contradictory or cautionary evidence (keep visible)

- **Text can hurt:** unfiltered news worsened forecasts (#6); text worsened RF and Elastic Net (#11); news sentiment gave mixed results (#10).
- **LLM forecasts can be weak:** experts beat the best LLM (#1); real-time LLM inflation forecasts were stale (#4); LLM agents were weakest on CPI and inflation in a live US test (#2, full text); all agents trailed the market (#3).
- **Memory or ledgers do not reliably help:** the Forecast-Dojo notebook improved 6 models and worsened 6 (#3).
- **Combination evidence conflicts:** equal weights won on a 6-month test (#9), while performance weights won on a long evaluation with significance tests (#7).
- **Leakage is real:** strong in-sample memorisation (#16). Several India papers used full-sample choices (#9, #11, #8).
